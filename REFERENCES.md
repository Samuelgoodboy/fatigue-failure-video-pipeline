 2. Academic Textbooks & Standards
1. **Callister, W. D., & Rethwisch, D. G.** (2018). *Materials Science and Engineering: An Introduction* (10th ed.). Wiley.
   * *Chapter 8: Failure* (Detailed explanations of fatigue, beach marks, striations, and the S-N endurance limit).
2. **Dowling, N. E.** (2012). *Mechanical Behavior of Materials: Engineering Methods for Deformation, Fracture, and Fatigue* (4th ed.). Pearson.
   * *Chapters 11-14: Fatigue Crack Growth and Linear Elastic Fracture Mechanics (LEFM).*
3. **ASM International** (1996). *ASM Handbook, Volume 19: Fatigue and Fracture*. ASM International.
   * *Reference for macro-fractography and failure analysis of metal specimens.*
